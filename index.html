Welcome to Bible Lesson Builder

You're looking at a sample lesson on 2 Peter 2. Every lesson comes together in three steps:

  1. Brainstorm — jot down ideas, questions and illustrations as they come.
  2. Scripture — look up verses and pull them into your outline.
  3. Presenter Mode — teach from your phone in full screen; the screen stays on while you teach.

Your work saves automatically in this browser. Sign in to keep it in sync across devices.
